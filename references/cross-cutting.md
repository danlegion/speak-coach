# Cross-Cutting Modules

Shared procedures every command uses.

## Transcript Metrics (compute before judging)
From the transcript + recording, compute first:
- **WPM:** words ÷ minutes. Target band 120–150; <110 sluggish, >160 rushed.
- **Fillers/min:** count um, uh, like, you know, sentence-initial so, basically, actually — ÷ minutes. <4 strong; 4–8 workable; >8 drill target.
- **Pauses:** count silences ≥1s; note placement (before key points = deliberate). 0 pauses in 60s+ = flag; 3+ deliberate = strong.
- **Run-ons:** longest stretch without a pause or sentence break; >25 words without a breath = flag.
- **Energy/articulation:** one judgment line from the recording (not the transcript), evidence-tagged: flat vs. varied, mumbled vs. crisp.

## Predict + Three-Stage Review Protocol
After every drill, before revealing the scorecard: "Before you review — predict what you'll see." Then review in three stages — **visual (mute)**: body and presence; **vocal (audio only)**: pace, volume, melody; **verbal (transcript)**: structure, repetition, fillers. At each stage the speaker names one observation before the coach adds theirs. Then the scorecard. Log the gap between their prediction and your scores — it feeds Calibration.

## Evidence Tags
No fake certainty. Tag contested claims High / Medium / Low confidence. Thin evidence (short sample, noisy transcript) gets named: "Low confidence — 40-second sample."

## One-Fix Rule
Every feedback block: strengths first (genuine, specific), then exactly one fix stated as a concrete behavior ("pause a full second before your key number"). Never a list of three. The fix becomes the immediate re-drill.

## Difficulty Dials
Apply per drill instead of maintaining separate advanced sets: **time shrink** (90s→60s→30s same prompt), **constraint stacking** (add one constraint per rep), **hostile follow-ups** ("Why should I care?", 20s to answer), **camera on** (audio reps graduate to video), **audience upgrade** (alone → live with coach → hostile Q&A).

## Audience Framing
Every drill declares an audience before it starts (exec review, team standup, stranger at a party, recruiter call…). The content scorecard's Audience-fit dimension is scored against it. Advanced variant: same material, three audiences, three different good versions.
