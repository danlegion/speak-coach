# Scoring Rubrics

Score each content dimension 1–5 with one line of evidence. Overall drill result: **Clear** (at/above the speaker's level bar) or **Re-drill** (below bar).

## Base dimensions (every track)
- **Point clarity:** one sentence could capture what they said. If you can't write that sentence, they didn't land it.
- **Structure:** framework present and complete (PREP: point, reason, example, point; STAR where relevant).
- **Conciseness:** nothing removable without loss. Flag throat-clearing openers ("So, basically, what I think is…").
- **Audience fit:** detail depth, jargon, and framing match the declared audience.
- **Emotional resonance:** storytelling reps — relive, don't report; present-tense sensory detail, felt emotion.

## Track-specific notes
- **Voice:** content rubric is light — score Point clarity + Conciseness only; the delivery metrics carry the drill.
- **Structure:** Structure dimension is double-weighted in the verdict; a complete framework with weak delivery still Clears at L1–L2.
- **Impromptu:** add **Composure** (no freeze, no trailing off, clean ending) as a fifth dimension; it gates Clear at every level.
- **Interview:** add **Differentiation** (L3+: quantified results + earned secret/spiky POV) and **Pushback handling** (L4+: direct answers, no collapse, no rambling).
- **Everyday:** score **Turn-taking** (no monologues, no dead air), **Question quality** (follow-ups build on specifics), **Disagreement handling** (L3+: clear position, warm tone, reasons), **Listening signals** (references what the partner said).
- **Stage:** add **Opening/closing** (deliberate hook, landing line) and **Q&A bridging** (L4+: answers stay short, bridges feel natural).

## Delivery scorecard bands (quick reference — ordered by causal leverage)
Pauses: 0 in 60s+ = flag, deliberate placement = strong (the master lever) · Rate: 120–150 overall, key lines slower = strong, one flat default rate = flag · Melody/tonality: varied = strong, monotone = flag, sentences landing low = certain · Volume: dynamic range = strong, one flat volume = flag · Fillers: <4/min strong, 4–8 workable, >8 drill target (symptom — fix pause first) · Articulation: crisp vs. mumbled, one evidence-tagged line · Presence (video reps): posture, open-zone gestures, face matching words, eye contact.
