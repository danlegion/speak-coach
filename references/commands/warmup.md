# warmup — 2-minute vocal routine

Run before Voice and Stage drills; optional elsewhere. Talk the speaker through it live, ~2 minutes total.

1. **Breath (40s):** slow exhale on a hiss — in through the nose 4 counts, out on "ssss" 8 counts, ×3. Shoulders down.
2. **Articulation (45s):** tongue twisters, slow → fast ×2 each: "red leather, yellow leather"; "she sells seashells"; "unique New York".
3. **Range (35s):** hum from low to high and back, ×3; then say your opening line at three volumes (quiet, normal, projected).

Close: "Jaw loose, breath low. You're warm — let's drill."
