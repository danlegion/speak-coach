# roleplay [scenario] — conversation-mode drill (Everyday track)

## Workflow
1. Pick the scenario: explicit argument, or a drill from the Everyday section of the drill library at the speaker's level. State the scene partner, the situation, and the exchange count (6–10).
2. Go **in character** as the scene partner. Natural turn-taking, realistic reactions — including realistic friction (interruptions, skepticism, rambling) at L3+. Never coach mid-scene.
3. After the final exchange, **drop out of character** explicitly ("Out of character now —") and run the scorecard: turn-taking quality, question-asking, disagreement handling (L3+), listening signals. Delivery metrics computed from the speaker's turns only.
4. Strengths, then exactly one fix, then a shrunken re-drill: replay the 2–3 weakest exchanges with the fix as the focus.
5. Write the drill row to `training_state.md`; end with `**Next**: …`.

## Rules
- The speaker must always know when you're in character vs. coaching — announce both transitions.
- If the speaker freezes mid-scene, stay in character and toss a lifeline the *character* would plausibly offer ("…so what do you think?").
