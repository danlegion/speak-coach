# Drill Library

Starter drills per track per level. The coach picks the drill matching the speaker's current level on the requested track; the third `Clear` toward a mastery gate must differ in prompt or difficulty dial from the first two. Every drill runs the Drill Loop (brief → respond → ingest & analyze → predict → review → dual scorecard → one fix → re-drill). Drills default to text mode (write in one go, content scored only); voice/video is the upgrade — see Response Modes in COACH.md.

Format per drill: **prompt**, time limit, constraints, and the **bar** (what `Clear` looks like at that level).

---

## Voice — delivery mechanics

### L1 — complete 60s without stopping
**V-L1-1 "Sixty Clean"** — Talk for 60 seconds about what you did this weekend. Time: 60s. Constraints: no stopping or restarting; a flubbed rep is data. Bar: reaches 60s without a restart.
**V-L1-2 "Read Aloud"** — Read a paragraph of your choice aloud, 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: no restarts, keep going through stumbles. Bar: finishes the time without stopping.

### L2 — 120–150 WPM, fillers <8/min across 90s
**V-L2-1 "Cruise Control"** — 90 seconds on a personal anecdote (a trip, a project). Time: 90s. Constraints: none yet. Bar: pace lands 120–150 WPM, fillers under 8/min.
**V-L2-2 "Steady Story"** — 90 seconds explaining something you know well (your job, a hobby). Time: 90s. Constraints: no sentence-initial "so". Bar: pace band held, fillers <8/min.

### L3 — fillers <4/min, 3+ deliberate pauses before key points
**V-L3-1 "Pause Placer"** — 90 seconds making a case for something (a tool, a decision). Time: 90s. Constraints: at least 3 pauses ≥1s, each placed right before a key point. Bar: fillers <4/min and all 3 pauses land before key points.
**V-L3-2 "Number Drop"** — 60 seconds delivering three numbers/stats that matter to you. Time: 60s. Constraints: full 1-second pause before each number. Bar: fillers <4/min, pauses audible before all three numbers.

### L4 — varied emphasis/intonation, pace adapts to content
**V-L4-1 "Gear Shift"** — 2 minutes on a topic with a story and a takeaway. Time: 2min. Constraints: slow down for the key lines, move quicker through context. Bar: pace audibly adapts; no filler clusters; emphasis sounds intentional, not performed.
**V-L4-2 "Cold Emphasis"** — 90 seconds, impromptu topic. Time: 90s. Constraints: mark the one line that matters with emphasis + a pause. Bar: intonation varies, the key line lands harder than the rest.

### L5 — sustain L4 across 3+ min, energy holds to the close
**V-L5-1 "The Closer"** — 3-minute talk on a topic you care about. Time: 3min. Constraints: L4 bars throughout; close with a landing line. Bar: no fade in the final 30s; pauses still deliberate at minute 3.
**V-L5-2 "Marathon Mic"** — 3+ minutes, topic of the coach's choice. Time: 3min+. Constraints: camera on. Bar: energy and articulation hold to the close; zero filler clusters.

## Structure — storytelling and frameworks

### L1 — states a clear point out loud
**S-L1-1 "One Point"** — 30 seconds: what do you think about remote work? Time: 30s. Constraints: none. Bar: the coach can write your point in one sentence.
**S-L1-2 "Headline Me"** — 30 seconds: headline + one reason for a recent decision you made. Time: 30s. Constraints: no throat-clearing opener. Bar: point is statable in one sentence.

### L2 — PREP or STAR skeleton without prompting
**S-L2-1 "PREP Rep"** — 60 seconds on a simple opinion (best phone you've owned). Time: 60s. Constraints: use PREP (point, reason, example, point) without being reminded mid-drill. Bar: all four PREP beats present and in order.
**S-L2-2 "STAR Short"** — 90 seconds on a work win. Time: 90s. Constraints: STAR skeleton. Bar: situation, task, action, result all present; ends on the result.

### L3 — complete framework with evidence and a landing line
**S-L3-1 "Full Build"** — 2 minutes recommending something (a book, a hire, a tool). Time: 2min. Constraints: complete framework + concrete evidence + a landing line. Bar: evidence is specific (names, numbers), landing line restates the point.
**S-L3-2 "Evidence Stack"** — 2 minutes: convince me of an unpopular opinion you hold. Time: 2min. Constraints: two concrete evidence pieces minimum. Bar: point survives the evidence test — nothing removable without loss.

### L4 — adapts the same material to 30s and 3min
**S-L4-1 "Accordion 30"** — Take your last S-L3 topic; deliver it in 30 seconds. Time: 30s. Constraints: same point, same evidence (compressed). Bar: point and at least one evidence survive; still lands.
**S-L4-2 "Accordion 180"** — Same topic, 3 minutes. Time: 3min. Constraints: expand without padding — add a second example or a counterargument addressed. Bar: the extra time adds substance, not filler.

### L5 — structures hostile/complex material on the fly, frameworks invisible
**S-L5-1 "Hostile Brief"** — 2 minutes on a complex topic you know (explained to a skeptic). Time: 2min. Constraints: no naming the framework out loud. Bar: structure is felt, not announced; skeptic's objection pre-answered.
**S-L5-2 "Cold Framework"** — Impromptu complex topic, 2 minutes. Time: 2min. Constraints: 10 seconds prep, then go. Bar: clear arc; no rambling; lands the point.

## Impromptu — thinking on your feet

### L1 — 30s on a familiar topic without freezing
**I-L1-1 "Warm Take"** — 30 seconds: your favorite meal and why. Time: 30s. Constraints: keep talking; silence over 3s = freeze. Bar: no freeze.
**I-L1-2 "Weekend Recap"** — 30 seconds: what you did last weekend. Time: 30s. Constraints: no freeze. Bar: reaches 30s with continuous speech.

### L2 — 60s on an unknown topic, no freeze or trailing off
**I-L2-1 "Mystery Card"** — Coach names an everyday topic (traffic, coffee, shoes). 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: no trailing off mid-sentence at the end. Bar: full 60s, ends on a complete sentence.
**I-L2-2 "News React"** — React to a headline for 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: take a position. Bar: position stated; no freeze; clean ending.

### L3 — 90s unknown topic, one clear point, fillers <4/min
**I-L3-1 "Point Finder"** — Unknown topic, 90 seconds. Time: 90s. Constraints: land exactly one clear point. Bar: one statable point; fillers <4/min.
**I-L3-2 "Odd One"** — Odd topic (why do we have eyebrows?). 90 seconds with visible structure. Time: 90s. Constraints: PREP or equivalent. Bar: structured; point lands; fillers <4/min.

### L4 — abstract/odd topics with visible structure
**I-L4-1 "Abstract Art"** — Abstract topic (what is fairness?). 90 seconds. Time: 90s. Constraints: define the term your way, then argue. Bar: definition + argument; structure visible throughout.
**I-L4-2 "Devil's Advocate"** — Defend a view you don't hold, 90 seconds. Time: 90s. Constraints: steelman it — no strawmen. Bar: a holder of that view would recognize the argument.

### L5 — 60s on hostile/absurd topic, lands point + memorable line
**I-L5-1 "Absurd Landing"** — Absurd topic (should pigeons pay rent?). 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: land the point AND one memorable line. Bar: point + a line worth quoting.
**I-L5-2 "Ambush"** — Topic revealed the moment recording starts. 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: zero prep. Bar: no freeze; point lands; memorable line present.

## Interview — answers under evaluation

### L1 — full sentences with a concrete example
**N-L1-1 "Full Sentence"** — "Tell me about yourself," 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: full sentences, include one concrete example. Bar: no fragments; example present.
**N-L1-2 "Example Drop"** — "Describe a project you led," 60 seconds. Time: 60s. Constraints: one concrete example with specifics. Bar: example has names/numbers, not generalities.

### L2 — STAR-complete inside 2.5 minutes
**N-L2-1 "STAR Clock"** — "Tell me about a difficult stakeholder," 2.5 minutes. Time: 2:30. Constraints: complete STAR, finish inside the clock. Bar: all four beats; ends by 2:30.
**N-L2-2 "Result First"** — "Tell me about a win," 2 minutes. Time: 2min. Constraints: lead with the quantified result. Bar: result in the first 20 seconds; STAR completes after.

### L3 — quantified results + differentiator
**N-L3-1 "Spiky Take"** — "Why should we hire you?", 2 minutes. Time: 2min. Constraints: quantified results + one differentiator (earned secret or spiky POV). Bar: numbers present; differentiator is specific to you, not generic.
**N-L3-2 "Differentiator"** — "What makes you different from other candidates?", 2 minutes. Time: 2min. Constraints: proof, not adjectives. Bar: every claim backed by evidence.

### L4 — handles follow-ups and pushback without collapsing
**N-L4-1 "Pushback"** — Answer "tell me about a failure" (2min), then handle 2 sharp follow-ups, 45s each. Time: 2min + 2×45s. Constraints: no rambling, no collapsing under pushback. Bar: follow-ups answered directly; composure holds.
**N-L4-2 "Curveball"** — Standard answer (90s), then one unexpected follow-up ("what would your manager say you do worst?"), 60s. Time: 90s + 60s. Constraints: answer the question asked, not the one you prepared. Bar: direct answer; no dodge; recovers cleanly.

### L5 — concise, memorable, controls the arc
**N-L5-1 "One Line"** — "Why this role?", 2 minutes ending with a memorable one-liner. Time: 2min. Constraints: the last line must be quotable. Bar: interviewer leaves with that one line.
**N-L5-2 "Arc Control"** — 3-minute arc across an opener + 2 follow-ups on one theme. Time: 3min total. Constraints: each answer advances the same theme. Bar: the three answers build; theme lands harder at the end than the start.

## Everyday — conversation as a skill

Conversation-mode drills: the coach plays the scene partner in character for the stated exchanges, then drops out of character for the scorecard (turn-taking, question-asking, disagreement handling, listening signals).

### L1 — 3-turn small-talk exchange without dying out
**E-L1-1 "Coffee Machine"** — In character: coworker at the coffee machine, 3 turns each. Constraints: no one-word answers. Bar: exchange doesn't die; ends naturally.
**E-L1-2 "Hello Again"** — In character: stranger at a party, 3 turns each. Constraints: ask at least one question. Bar: conversation stays alive past your turns.

### L2 — genuine follow-ups, visible listening
**E-L2-1 "Follow-Up"** — 6 turns each; partner mentions a weekend trip. Constraints: ask genuine follow-up questions (not interrogation). Bar: at least 3 follow-ups that build on what was said.
**E-L2-2 "Curious Three"** — 6 turns each on the partner's hobby. Constraints: three follow-up questions minimum. Bar: questions show you listened (reference specifics they said).

### L3 — polite disagreement with reasons, no hedging into mush
**E-L3-1 "Kind No"** — Partner proposes a plan you think is bad. 4–6 turns. Constraints: disagree with reasons; no "maybe, I guess, sort of". Bar: position clear; tone warm; reasons concrete.
**E-L3-2 "Disagree Light"** — Meeting disagreement, 4–6 turns. Constraints: one clear reason, one alternative offered. Bar: disagreement voiced without apology-padding; alternative on the table.

### L4 — steers a meandering conversation back on track
**E-L4-1 "Shepherd"** — Partner rambles across three topics; 8 turns. Constraints: bring it back to the original question gracefully. Bar: conversation returns to the point without the partner feeling cut off.
**E-L4-2 "Redirect"** — Colleague vents for 4 turns; you need a decision. 6–8 turns. Constraints: acknowledge, then redirect to the decision. Bar: acknowledgment feels real; decision gets made.

### L5 — defuses tension, handles awkwardness with ease
**E-L5-1 "Defuse"** — Partner is visibly annoyed about a missed deadline (yours). 8–10 turns. Constraints: no groveling, no defensiveness. Bar: tension drops; next step agreed; dignity intact on both sides.
**E-L5-2 "Tension Cut"** — Awkward moment: you forgot the partner's name mid-conversation. 6–8 turns. Constraints: recover with ease, no over-apologizing. Bar: moment passes; conversation continues warmer than before.

## Stage — presence and persuasion

### L1 — 2-minute talk to camera without reading
**T-L1-1 "Two Minutes"** — 2 minutes to camera on something you know well. Time: 2min. Constraints: camera on, no reading, no notes. Bar: reaches 2 minutes without reading.
**T-L1-2 "Camera Debut"** — 2 minutes to camera on a familiar opinion. Time: 2min. Constraints: look at the lens. Bar: full 2 minutes, eyes on lens, no script.

### L2 — clear opening and closing, energy holds
**T-L2-1 "Bookends"** — 2-minute talk. Time: 2min. Constraints: deliberate opening line + deliberate closing line. Bar: opening earns attention; closing lands; energy doesn't dip mid-talk.
**T-L2-2 "Open Strong"** — 90 seconds: opening that earns attention, clean close. Time: 90s. Constraints: first 10 seconds must hook. Bar: hook works; close is a full sentence, not a fade.

### L3 — persuasive arc with a specific call to action
**T-L3-1 "The Ask"** — 3 minutes persuading the audience to do one specific thing. Time: 3min. Constraints: one CTA, stated twice (middle + close). Bar: CTA is specific and actionable; arc builds to it.
**T-L3-2 "Persuade Me"** — 2 minutes: convince me to change one habit. Time: 2min. Constraints: address the obvious objection. Bar: objection pre-answered; CTA clear.

### L4 — handles Q&A without rambling, bridges back
**T-L4-1 "Q&A Guard"** — 2-minute talk + 3 audience questions, 45s answers. Time: 2min + 3×45s. Constraints: answer then bridge back to your point. Bar: answers under 45s; bridges feel natural, not evasive.
**T-L4-2 "Bridge Back"** — Give a 60-second position, then one off-topic question: bridge back in 30 seconds. Time: 60s + 30s. Constraints: acknowledge the question first. Bar: acknowledgment + bridge inside 30s; point restated.

### L5 — commands a 5-minute talk plus hostile Q&A
**T-L5-1 "Hostile Room"** — 5-minute talk + 3 hostile questions, 60s answers. Time: 5min + 3×60s. Constraints: camera on; no notes. Bar: composure under hostility; answers direct; room controlled.
**T-L5-2 "Final Boss Talk"** — 5 minutes on a topic the coach assigns + 3 hostile follow-ups. Time: 5min + 3×60s. Constraints: 5 minutes prep, then go. Bar: talk has an arc; Q&A handled without rambling or retreating.
